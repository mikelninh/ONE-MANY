# ONE / MANY – Visitor experiment 001

Proposed formative usability study, n=5 independent visitors. Not yet conducted.

## Procedure
1. Invite five volunteers who have not worked on the project. Explain that participation is voluntary; do not collect names or sensitive details.
2. Ask participants to open visit-test.html. Do not explain how the museum works. If observing live, ask permission and avoid coaching.
3. Observe whether each visitor can discover a story, find a different theme in the Atlas, and voluntarily form a concrete next step. Record completion, navigation friction and whether help was required. Use a timer only with permission.
4. Ask the visitor to export their optional feedback and send it themselves. No server collection or tracking is present.
5. Aggregate task completion, independent success, median clarity and visual scores, remembered stories, and action intentions. Separate intention from actual completed action. Discuss failure cases and improve the next iteration.

## Provisional release criteria
At least four of five can find and open a story without assistance; at least four of five can find another topic; median clarity and visual rating at least 4/5. Action intent is exploratory, not a condition for participation. This small sample is qualitative, not representative, and cannot demonstrate societal impact.

## Follow-up
Optional, consent-based follow-up after seven days: did the participant attempt the proposed action, what did they learn, and what would they change? Do not infer completion from an exported draft.
