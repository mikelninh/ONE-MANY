# The Science of Good — research protocol v0.1

Purpose: investigate what helps individuals and communities improve lives, including nonhuman animals, without assuming that famous successes reveal causal laws.

## Sample and limitations
Current archive: 64 purposively selected cases, 28 source pointers, 3 expanded descriptive cases. Source pointers are not independent validation. No completed original impact studies. Include less visible, unsuccessful, stalled, mixed and harmful cases in future sampling; document inclusion/exclusion and selection bias. The 10,000-case target is aspirational.

## Record structure
Case identifier, actors and affected groups, context and time, initial challenge, resources, action, observed outcomes (units and dates), source provenance, plausible mechanism, alternative explanations, counterevidence, unintended consequences, transfer conditions, next verification step. Distinguish reported outputs from outcomes, and attribution from contribution. Missing fields are unknown, not zero. Never combine unlike units into a generic impact ranking.

## Evidence workflow
1. Record claim and primary source; preserve date, exact location and attribution.
2. Seek independent corroboration and counterevidence; distinguish source type, relevance and bias.
3. Extract observed outcome and limitations; explicitly mark uncertainty.
4. Compare cases with similar problems, including failed cases; formulate falsifiable hypotheses, not universal recipes.
5. Design voluntary small experiments; predefine outcome, baseline when known, target, time window, comparison where feasible, and harms/balancing measures. Collect only necessary data with consent.
6. Review results, document failures, adapt and share deidentified findings only with permission. Independent review precedes any public causal claim.

## Pilot question
Can participants solve a subsequent comparable challenge more independently after a learning intervention? Proposed measures: proportion independently completing the next task, quality, time, assistance needed, adverse effects. Use a comparison condition if feasible, record contextual differences, and avoid inferring population effects from small samples. No pilot results exist.

## Privacy and implementation
Research notes use browser local storage separate from project state. Export is user-initiated and includes private notes: review before sharing. No backend, automated verification, external data collection, or live AI. Experiment drafts transfer to the existing workshop; a draft is not evidence of action or impact.
