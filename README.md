# ONE / MANY

**The Art of Changing the World.**  
Die Welt ist noch nicht fertig.

An independent, open research and product prototype for human agency and collective learning.

## Experience v4.0 — The Museum of Human Possibility
- Discover: 64 short portraits across eight themes, including three expanded case studies.
- Understand: practical learning hypotheses, methods, source leads, and a research notebook.
- Become: three-step guided experiment creation, private project workspace, official democratic participation routes, and local outcome documentation.
- 24 archival image references and 64 editorial fallback covers. The three lead image source pages and licenses were checked; not every image or story has been independently reviewed.
- Personal drafts and notes are stored in the browser, not synced to a server. JSON exports are available. No accounts or live AI.

## Research mission
Study 10,000 comparable cases over time, including failures and counterevidence. Proposed pilot: five individuals and five communities, 100 days of consent-based experimentation; assess whether participants can solve a subsequent problem more independently. These are proposed goals, not achieved results.

## Status and limitations
The source includes a working local prototype. Syntax and core simulated JavaScript workflows have been checked. Photos are loaded from Wikimedia; attribution and license links are shown beside the photographs. Editorial fallback graphics are original typographic designs, not historical images. No official partnership is claimed. Code licensing and contribution governance remain to be agreed.

## Design definition
A digital museum, not a feed. Four rooms: curated historical exhibitions, the 64-case atlas, a guided personal workshop, and a research laboratory. A cinematic dark entrance introduces an original 64-point symbolic constellation (not an empirical network), followed by light exhibition spaces. Motion is restrained, respects reduced-motion preferences, and is never required for access. Historical photographs retain their aspect ratio; the illustrated fallback remains visible until the photograph loads. All facts retain source links and uncertainty labels. The design aims at high-end exhibition quality; no awards or independent design rating are claimed.

## Grand Entrance / Living Atlas
The light museum entrance provides two exhibitions and eight themed rooms. atlas.html has all 64 cases with search, place, epoch and mechanism filters, gallery/chronological view, random discovery, local bookmarks, shareable story links and the existing archive reader/action handoff. Mechanisms are editorial hypotheses; epoch uses the first stated year and broad labels where needed. Existing 24 image references and 64 unique illustrated fallback motifs are reused. The two exhibition pages and existing local data are preserved. No new empirical evaluation or full image verification is claimed.

## Visitor experiment 001
visit-test.html is a voluntary, no-tracking visitor feedback form with local JSON export. docs/visitor-study.md defines a proposed five-person formative study. No participants have been recruited or results collected.

## The Science of Good / v0.1
science.html compares all 64 cases, displays three expanded case records, separates descriptive evidence and causal hypotheses, supports a private counterevidence notebook, JSON exports and three editable experiment drafts that transfer to the existing workshop. Research protocol: docs/research-protocol.md. No empirical impact study or automated source verification is claimed.

## Your Move / guided learning companion
Your Move is a four-step, rule-based private experiment designer for eight scales. It provides generic method prompts, explicit alternatives, safeguards, an outcome measure and a disconfirmation criterion; drafts resume locally and can be exported or transferred to the existing workshop. It is not a live AI assistant. Existing four embedded Cinema artworks are reused in Your Move and the Science of Good; the three deep cases use their associated symbolic scenes, other cases use the existing credited references and fallback illustrations. No new historical image rights or causal claims are made.
